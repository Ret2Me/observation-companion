Observation Companion is a mobile assistant for amateur radio operators and
satellite observers who work with the SatNOGS ecosystem.

Set your ground station location and antenna bands, and the app predicts upcoming
satellite passes, ranks them, and helps you decide which ones are worth tracking.

Features:

* Upcoming pass predictions (AOS/LOS, max elevation, azimuth) computed on-device
  with an SGP4/SDP4 orbit propagator.
* Sky-view chart and ground-track map for each pass (OpenStreetMap tiles).
* Doppler drift curve for the pass.
* Reception probability based on historical SatNOGS observations.
* Filtering by antenna band and by satellite.
* Observatory presets - save and reuse ground-station setups.
* Optional countdown alarms before a pass begins.
* Catalog and transmitter statistics from the SatNOGS database.

The app reads public data from the SatNOGS Database and Network APIs and from
CelesTrak (TLEs). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Libre Space
Foundation / SatNOGS project.
